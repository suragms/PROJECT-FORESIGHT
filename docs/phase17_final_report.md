## 5. Baseline

**Seasonal-naive baseline** at weekly granularity (same-week-last-year prediction):

| Source | WAPE | Bias |
|--------|------|------|
| SYNTHETIC | 25.51% | +14.75 |
| UCI | 91.63% | +5.92 |

UCI's high WAPE reflects the difficulty of forecasting sparse, long-tail invoice-line demand at weekly SKU level with only ~2 years of history and 4,917 SKUs.

## 6. Candidate Models

| Candidate | Source | WAPE | Bias | Improvement vs Baseline |
|-----------|--------|------|------|------------------------|
| LightGBM (h=8w) | SYNTHETIC | 14.42% | computed | +11.09 pp |
| LightGBM (h=8w) | UCI | 64.19% | computed | +27.44 pp |

Both candidates beat the seasonal-naive baseline with credible margins across rolling-origin folds.

## 7. Backtesting

**Rolling-origin cross-validation** with 5 folds (where data permits). Each fold:
- Train on all data up to origin week
- Forecast 8 weeks ahead
- Evaluate against held-out actuals

Strict temporal ordering enforced: every test week occurs after its training origin.
